How Hi-Lo counting works (cheat sheet)

1. Tag every card you see

CardsValueWhy
2, 3, 4, 5, 6+1Good for the dealer
7, 8, 90Neutral
10, J, Q, K, A−1Good for you

Start at 0 after every shuffle. Add each card you see, including the dealer’s hole card once it is turned over. Tip: cancel pairs as you go (a low and a high card cancel out).

2. Turn it into a true count

True count = running count ÷ decks left. Estimate decks left by looking at the discard tray, to the nearest half deck.

Running +6 with 3 decks left is a true count of +2. A positive count means the shoe is rich in tens and aces, which favors you.

Each true-count point is worth about +0.5% to the player. The house edge with basic strategy is about 0.5%, so you break even near +1.

3. Bet by the true count

True countBet
+1 or lower1 unit (table minimum)
+22 units
+34 units
+46 units
+5 or higher8 units

The true count is rounded down. Raise bets when the count is high, drop back to the minimum when it is not.

Rules in this game

  • Dealer stands on all 17s and peeks for blackjack.
  • Blackjack pays 3 to 2. Insurance pays 2 to 1.
  • Double on any first two cards, including after a split.
  • Split up to 4 hands. Split aces get one card each and can’t be re-split.
  • No surrender. Play is fake money only.

How to use the two modes

  1. Training shows the count, card values, deck percentages, the best bet and the best play. Play until the numbers feel natural.
  2. Testing hides all of it. After each hand you get graded on your bet, every play, and a running-count check.
